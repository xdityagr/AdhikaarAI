What you get in a minute

✓

Every scheme you qualify for

Not just one. You often qualify for three, and they do not cost the same — on a ₹1.2 lakh project the gap between the cheapest and the dearest is about ₹29,000. We put the cheapest first.

₹

What it truly costs

Not just the instalment — the total you repay, and how that compares to the moneylender you would otherwise use. That comparison is usually the moment it becomes real.

◎

An office that can actually pay

Applications get sent to agencies that cannot disburse right now. We check NSFDC's own published fund data first, and tell you plainly when an office has been ruled out.

Ask in your own words

No forms to decode. Type or tap, in Hindi, English, Marathi, Bengali or Tamil — write in your own script and it follows you there automatically.

☰

Six questions, tappable

Every question has options you can tap. Typing is allowed, never required.

◐

Explains the misses too

You are told which schemes you did not qualify for and exactly which threshold you missed. That is the part people keep.

⚑

Free, and it says so

No government scheme charges a fee to apply. If anyone asks you for money to process or approve your application, that is fraud — and the app tells you that every time.
